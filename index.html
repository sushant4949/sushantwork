Highlight of 2026

Some stories travel farther than you expect. And sometimes, they take you to the iconic red carpet at Cannes.

In June 2026, I found myself at the Cannes Lions International Festival of Creativity as part of the ERA Cohort and took along a story close to home, Mothers of Courage, a Shikshagraha film produced by Mantra4Change, which became India’s only SDG Lions shortlist at Cannes Lions.

What did I do at Cannes?

Five days were nowhere near enough. I watched 80 films, sat through 30 sessions, met the makers behind some of the world’s most exciting campaigns, learned from creative minds across the industry and made a few friends across borders.
